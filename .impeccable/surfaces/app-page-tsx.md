# Surface: public landing page (Persuade)

Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience is the whole Capabilio ecosystem: students and recent graduates, working professionals and executives (both coming soon), organisations (colleges, universities, companies), and anyone verifying a Skill Passport. A first-time visitor should leave with the complete picture. Positioning (user-decided): a career operating system for India and the rest of the world where proof matters rather than resume and portfolio. Spelling: "resume", "Skill Passport". No invented customers, counts, testimonials or compliance badges; demonstration data is labelled "Sample data"; external quotes and figures are real and linked to their source.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Capabilio is an operating system for proof. Skills are terrain that evidence raises, and every path and app writes to one Skill Passport ledger. The page refuses the centered-headline-plus-dashboard-screenshot landing.
OWN-WORLD: Daybreak plus OS. Periwinkle sky falling to apricot and brand orange, layered ridges from far rose to near night-indigo (#121634), ink navy type, Bricolage Grotesque display, Geist body, JetBrains Mono for data only. Product mock-ups are crisp white windows with real shadows. Night sections carry the OS metaphor: an orbiting ecosystem map, a desktop with a glass dock of eight apps, a live proof stream, a command palette. Orange is action, indigo is evidence.
STORY: The world is moving from claims to proof (verified quotes and sourced figures), Capabilio is one ecosystem (four paths, eight apps, one Skill Passport), here is the OS in use, here is why the numbers can be trusted, and here is how to start as a student or as an organisation.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Sky gradient, headline "Resumes tell. Proof wins." top-left, one supporting paragraph, two buttons, a live skill-peak card top-right, and the lower terrain with six labelled skill peaks and a state legend, with a slow scan line crossing it.
FORM: Scroll story: hero terrain, two evidence marquees, manifesto, verified quotes and sourced facts, orbiting ecosystem map, Capabilio OS desktop with dock, four path cards, night trust section with proof stream and playable ELO ledger, organisations, dawn close. Cross-cutting: staggered entrance, scroll reveals, ridge parallax, Cmd/Ctrl+K palette. Seed: user-pinned (sarvam.ai reference), no roll.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
