# Surface: careers (Persuade)

Scope: /careers and /careers/[slug]. Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: people deciding whether to apply to Capabilio. Action: send proof of work. Constraints: all copy and behaviour unchanged; roles come from the database; the Daybreak landing (sky gradient, ridgeline canvas, night-indigo bands, pills) must not appear here; own header and footer.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Hiring here is a design-school crit: your work is pinned to a wall and read. The page refuses the landing's atmosphere-and-pills look and the generic job board of cards.
OWN-WORLD: Concrete-grey foam-board ground (#e3e4e0), white sheets pinned with drafting-tape strips, black masking-tape label strips, vermilion grease-pencil marks (#e0361b) drawn as SVG circles and underlines, ink #111. Archivo with width axis (expanded heavy display, normal body), mono only for data. Square 2px corners, ruled schedules, ink-inverse hover. Intro section is flat black with one white sheet pinned on it.
STORY: Proof is read, not filtered. The visitor sees how hiring works, finds a role in a plain schedule, and sends work with a few honest lines.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim black title-block header. Huge two-line Archivo headline left, light then heavy. Right: the sample "Apply with proof" sheet pinned with tape, rotated, a red pencil circle on "Send my proof" and a red underline on "Resume: optional". Black label-tape strip carries the open-role status. Primary action: See open roles.
FORM: Pin-up wall, position 5 of the grounded list, seed 00e63a4c. Sequence sheets, ruled drawing-schedule for principles and roles, pinned form sheet.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
